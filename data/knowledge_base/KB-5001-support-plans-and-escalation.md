# Support plans, response times and escalation

## First response targets

| Plan | P1 Critical | P2 High | P3 Normal | Channels |
|------|-------------|---------|-----------|----------|
| Personal | Not available | 2 business days | 3 business days | Email |
| Team | 4 hours | 8 business hours | 1 business day | Email, chat |
| Enterprise | 1 hour, 24/7 | 4 hours | 8 business hours | Email, chat, phone |

## Priority definitions
- **P1 Critical:** production stopped, security incident, or data loss in progress.
- **P2 High:** a major function is failing and no workaround exists.
- **P3 Normal:** questions, or problems with a workaround.

## Always escalate to a human
- Suspected account compromise or ransomware.
- Mass deletion needing a library rollback.
- Swollen batteries, overheating or burning smells.
- Data recovery from a failed drive.
- Refunds, billing disputes or legal questions.

## How to escalate
Open a ticket at support.veloria.example, or use the phone number in your account portal for Enterprise plans. Include the product version, operating system, error code and steps tried.
