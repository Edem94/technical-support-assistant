# Reset your Veloria ID password or replace a lost MFA device

## Reset a password
1. Go to id.veloria.example and select **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your email and open the reset link. It is valid for 30 minutes.
3. Choose a new password of at least 14 characters. You cannot reuse your last 5 passwords.

## Lost phone or MFA device
1. On the sign-in page select **Use a recovery code** and enter one of the 10 codes saved when you set up MFA.
2. Open **Security > Two-step verification** and register your new device.

## No recovery codes
- **Business accounts:** your admin can reset MFA from the admin console.
- **Personal accounts:** open a support ticket for an identity check. It takes 1 to 3 business days.

Support agents and this assistant can never ask for your password, MFA code or recovery code. Never share these with anybody.
