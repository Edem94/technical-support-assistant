# Veloria ID sign-in errors and account lockouts

## ID-401: Invalid credentials
Check the email and password. Personal and work accounts are separate. Reset the password through KB-4001 if necessary.

## ID-423: Account locked
After 10 failed attempts in 15 minutes, the account locks for 30 minutes. Wait for the lock to end or reset your password, which clears the lock immediately. Admins can also unlock users.

## ID-429: Too many MFA attempts
Wait 15 minutes. If you did not start these requests, **do not approve them**: someone may have your password. Reset your password right away and report it through KB-5001.

## ID-451: Blocked by organization policy
Your organization restricts sign-in by device or location. Only your admin can change this.
