# VeloBook warranty and repairs

## Coverage
- **Standard warranty:** 2 years from purchase, covering manufacturing defects.
- **Battery:** covered for 1 year, or if capacity is below 80% within the warranty period and under 500 cycles.
- **VeloCare Plus (optional):** extends coverage to 4 years and adds one accidental damage repair per year, with a USD 49 service fee.

Accidental damage, liquid damage and damage from unauthorized repair are not covered by the standard warranty.

## Start a repair
1. Find your serial number on the base of the laptop or in **Veloria Control Center > About**.
2. Open a repair request in your account portal.
3. Back up your data. Repairs may erase the drive.
4. Print the prepaid label and ship the laptop. Typical turnaround is 5 to 7 business days.

Veloria does not offer data recovery as part of warranty repairs.
