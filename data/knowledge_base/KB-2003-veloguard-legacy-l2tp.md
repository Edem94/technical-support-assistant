# Configure VeloGuard 5 with L2TP/IPsec

**DEPRECATED: VeloGuard 5 reached end of life on 2025-12-31. Do not follow these steps on VeloGuard 6. Use KB-2001.**

1. Open **Network settings** and add a new L2TP/IPsec VPN.
2. Set the server to `vpn-legacy.veloria.example`.
3. Enter the pre-shared key from your IT admin.
4. For error VG-619, open UDP ports 500, 1701 and 4500 on your router.
