# VeloBook will not turn on

1. Connect the original 65 W USB-C charger to the left-rear port and wait 30 minutes. A fully drained battery may not show any light at first.
2. Unplug all accessories, including docks and USB drives.
3. Perform a **power reset**: hold the power button for 30 seconds, release it, wait 10 seconds, then press it once.
4. Look at the power LED. If it blinks in a pattern, see the LED table in the VeloBook 14 User Guide (PDF) and error codes VB-E01 to VB-E09.

## Black screen but the fans are running
Connect an external monitor. If an image appears, the internal display or its cable has failed; see KB-5002 about warranty repair.

## Swollen battery
If the case is bulging, or the touchpad is raised, **stop using the laptop, do not charge it, and contact support immediately**. Do not attempt to open it.
